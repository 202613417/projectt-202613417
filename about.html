About ShopNow

ShopNow is a university e-commerce project for browsing technology products, managing a shopping cart, and placing an order after signing in.

The catalog and orders use Supabase. Your cart stays in your browser until you place an order or remove its items.

Checkout simulates payment for learning purposes. No real payment is collected.
